Secure your controller

A password keeps other people and devices on your Wi‑Fi out of your heater settings.

Without a password, anyone on your home Wi‑Fi can change your heater settings. To update firmware, factory reset or change Wi‑Fi you will have to hold the BOOT button on the controller for 3 seconds. You can add a password later in Settings.

The hotspot is the controller's own Wi‑Fi. It is only needed if your home Wi‑Fi is down or changes.
